Built for real output quality

Every detail that makes exported documents actually usable.

Smart noise removal

Mozilla Readability extracts only the article content — ads, navigation, cookie banners, sidebars, and comment sections are stripped before conversion.

Tables with borders

Tables are preserved with proper cell borders, header shading, and padding. No more borderless cells or collapsed columns in Word.

Real Puppeteer PDF

PDFs are generated server-side by a headless Chrome instance — real text, real page breaks, proper A4 margins, and searchable content. Not a screenshot.

Full formatting preserved

Headings, bold, italic, underline, inline code, blockquotes, hyperlinks, superscript, and subscript all carry over correctly in both DOCX and PDF.
