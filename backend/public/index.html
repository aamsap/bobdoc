Part 1

Installing Bobdoc in Chrome

Chrome extensions from outside the Web Store are loaded as "unpacked". It takes under a minute.

  1. 1
    Download the extension

    Click Download Extension and save the .zip file anywhere on your computer.

  2. 2
    Unzip the file

    Right-click the downloaded .zip and choose Extract All (Windows) or double-click (Mac). You'll get a folder — keep note of where it is.

  3. 3
    Open Chrome extensions page

    In Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions or go to Menu → Extensions → Manage Extensions.

  4. 4
    Enable Developer mode

    Toggle on Developer mode in the top-right corner of the extensions page. A new toolbar will appear.

  5. 5
    Load the extension

    Click Load unpacked, then select the folder you extracted in step 2. Bobdoc will appear in your extensions list.

  6. 6
    Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle icon ⚙ in Chrome's toolbar and click the pin icon next to Bobdoc so it's always one click away.

Part 2

Exporting a page

Once installed, exporting any article is instant.

  1. 1
    Navigate to any article or page

    Open the webpage you want to export in Chrome — a news article, blog post, documentation page, or report.

  2. 2
    Click the Bobdoc icon

    Click the Bobdoc icon in your Chrome toolbar. A small popup appears with two buttons.

  3. 3
    Choose your format

    Click Download DOCX to get a Word-compatible document, or Download PDF for a print-ready PDF. Bobdoc automatically strips ads, sidebars, and navigation — only the article content is exported.

  4. 4
    Your file downloads

    The file saves to your default Downloads folder. The filename is taken from the page title. Open it in Word, Google Docs, or any PDF reader.

Bobdoc works best on article-style pages. Very dynamic pages (e.g. Twitter feeds, interactive maps) may not extract correctly as they have no single article body.
